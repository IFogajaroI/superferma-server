# Terminology

- `FarmField`: server-side representation of a farm cell.
- `field_id`: Request field 7 in farm actions.
- `water_amount`: FarmField field 2, fixed32 float.
- `status`: FarmField field 6, string.
- `MowMachine`: mower state returned by the server.
- `seed_packet_id`: Request field 12 for ACTION 204; Response field 5 for seed purchase flow.
- `flag_id`: Request field 29, observed in ACTION 404 after city entry.
